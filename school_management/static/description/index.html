School Management

Admissions, academics, exams, fees and parents in one Odoo 19 app, built for Kenyan schools (CBC and 8-4-4)

Run the whole school year in Odoo: enrol students, plan classes and timetables, take attendance, record exams and publish report cards, invoice fees through Odoo Accounting, and keep parents informed on the parent portal, by email and by SMS.

Key Features

Admissions and students

  • Application workflow: submit, accept, enrol.
  • Student records with NEMIS UPI, birth certificate, medical and special-needs notes.
  • Guardians with roles (primary contact, fee payer, emergency); siblings share one guardian.
  • Class history, promotion and a guided year-end rollover with repeaters.

Academics and exams

  • Classes and streams, subjects and teachers per class.
  • Weekly timetable grid with clash detection and A4 PDF print.
  • Daily attendance registers per class.
  • Mark entry with the CBC 8-level and KCSE grade scales, weighted report cards with class positions.

Fees on Odoo Accounting

  • Fee structures per grade level, term and day/boarding.
  • Term invoices for whole classes, billed to the fee-paying guardian, with bursary discounts and transport fees.
  • Instalments, advance payments and credit applied automatically.
  • Running fee balance per student and a printable fee statement.

Parents and communication

  • Parent portal: children, fee balance, invoices, report cards and fee statements.
  • Announcements to all parents, grade levels or classes, with attachments.
  • Private guardian conversations with replies from the portal.
  • SMS announcements, message alerts and fee balance reminders.

School operations

  • Teaching and non-teaching staff on Odoo Employees, with school roles.
  • Library books and loans with overdue fines.
  • Transport routes and subscriptions.
  • Hostel rooms and allocations.

Dashboard and roles

  • Dashboard with enrolment, attendance, fee collection and items needing attention.
  • Separate roles for teachers, registrar, bursar, librarian and administrator.
  • School name and logo on report cards, invoices and the portal.
  • Multi-company ready.

Getting Started

  1. Install School Management from Apps. Mail, Employees, Invoicing, Portal and SMS are installed with it.
  2. Give staff their school roles under Settings > Users, in the School section.
  3. Under School > Configuration, create the academic year and terms, subjects and lesson periods.
  4. Create classes, add fee structures, then enrol students from Admissions.
  5. Grant guardians portal access so they can follow their children online.

SMS use Odoo's standard SMS service. Optional add-ons from the same author send SMS through local gateways (SMS Gateways) and match M-Pesa Paybill payments to students (School M-Pesa Fee Payments).
